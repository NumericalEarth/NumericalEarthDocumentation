Freshwater discharge

River runoff and land-ice discharge reach the ocean through prescribed land components. Which ones to use depends on the atmospheric forcing:

  • JRA55-do: use JRA55PrescribedLand alone. It already includes Greenland's liquid runoff and solid ice discharge.
  • ERA5: combine ERA5PrescribedAtmosphere with GloFASPrescribedLand for rivers and GEUSPrescribedLand for Greenland's solid ice discharge. ERA5 itself includes no Greenland discharge.

1 km³/yr of water is about 1 Gt/yr, or 32 m³ s⁻¹. Typical magnitudes are rough literature values.

Solid ice discharge enters the ocean as the icebergs freshwater component. Unlike liquid runoff, it also takes from the ocean the latent heat needed to melt it (latent_heat_of_fusion in ComponentInterfaces, 334 kJ kg⁻¹ by default). It melts where it reaches the coast; icebergs do not drift.
